Services and useful links

  • Exoplanet Transit Database (opens in a new tab)

    A database of transit light curves with predictions of upcoming transits, run by the Variable Star and Exoplanet Section of the Czech Astronomical Society.

  • ExoClock (opens in a new tab)

    A project in which professional and amateur observers keep the transit times of the planets the Ariel space mission will observe up to date.

  • NASA Exoplanet Archive (opens in a new tab)

    A catalogue of confirmed exoplanets with their parameters and data from missions such as Kepler and TESS.
